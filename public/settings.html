1. Set up staff and accounts

PEOPLE & ACCESS

Keep the teacher directory and sign-in accounts in sync. They are separate records: a teacher must exist in the directory before their attendance can be recorded.

  1. Open Teachers and add each staff member to the directory.
  2. From Super Admin, create login accounts and choose a role.
  3. Users sign in with their username and password; the app opens a role-specific area.
Teacher directory Manage accounts

Removing a teacher also removes attendance entries linked to that teacher. Confirm before deleting a directory record.

2. Record daily attendance

CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT

Use Attendance to select a staff member and submit an attendance action. The app records the current date and time.

  1. Select the staff member, choose Check-in, and confirm.
  2. When leaving, choose the same staff member and submit Check-out.
  3. Each person can have one check-in and one check-out per day. Check-out requires a check-in first.
Open attendance

3. Review and export

DAILY & MONTHLY RECORDS

Reports read the saved attendance entries and let administrators review activity by date or month.

  • Use the attendance report to inspect and search saved records.
  • Use the monthly report to choose a month and filter by teacher.
  • Download monthly results as CSV or PDF for sharing or filing.
Attendance report Monthly report

4. Messages and notifications

KEEP THE TEAM INFORMED

Notifications are available from the bell controls on the app pages. The attendance screen also includes a help desk for reporting issues.

  • Super admins can send a notification to the system.
  • Teachers can send a problem report to administrators or send a note to the super admin.
  • Use the notification list to review messages; super admins can remove notifications.

Roles and day-to-day checks

ADMINISTRATION NOTES

Account roles

  • Teacher: signs in to the teacher area; the attendance screen is used to record staff attendance.
  • Admin: works from the admin dashboard to manage daily operations and reports.
  • Super Admin: manages accounts and system-wide notifications.

Before closing the day

  • Check that each staff member who arrived has a check-in.
  • Follow up on anyone whose report still shows no check-out.
  • Export the monthly CSV or PDF when a report needs to be archived.
